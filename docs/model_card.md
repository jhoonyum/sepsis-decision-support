# Model card: 24-hour sepsis progression risk (D1)

Follows the structure of applied model cards used in health-system model registries, with a TRIPOD+AI cross-reference at the end. Performance figures are not typed here: they are generated into the run's `evaluation_report.md` and linked below, so this card cannot drift from the results.

**Status:** template, v0.1. The model is currently fitted on synthetic data; sections marked *v0.2* are completed when the MIMIC-IV results are released.

## Model details

| | |
|---|---|
| Name | Sepsis Decision Support, landmark model D1 |
| Version | 0.1 (synthetic) |
| Type | Penalised (L2) logistic regression on stacked decision times; one model per stage (pre-shock, shock stage) and per number (24-hour risk now, next hour, 24-hour risk from one hour on) |
| Developer | Independent portfolio project; see CITATION.cff |
| Code | this repository; every run records the code revision and settings fingerprint |

## Intended use

- **Purpose:** research and teaching. Shows how a calibrated deterioration risk can be presented to clinicians together with the limits of what observational data can say about treatment.
- **Intended users:** researchers, data scientists and clinicians studying clinical prediction tools.
- **Population:** adults on their first ICU stay with Sepsis-3 recognised within 24 hours of ICU admission, excluding cardiac and thoracic surgery services; decision times from recognition up to 72 hours while the patient is alive and in the ICU.

## Out of scope

- Any decision about a real patient. The model is not a medical device and has not been evaluated prospectively.
- Use as an alarm or alert.
- Estimating the effect of a treatment or of its timing.
- Children, patients outside the ICU, cardiac or thoracic surgery patients, and sepsis recognised more than 24 hours after ICU admission.

## Inputs and outputs

- **Inputs** at each decision time: the latest value, its age, its change since the latest value at least 6 hours older (taken within the last 24 hours; missing otherwise) and its 24-hour measurement count for heart rate, mean and systolic blood pressure, respiratory rate, SpO2, temperature, GCS, lactate, creatinine, platelets, bilirubin and white cells; treatments running (vasopressor and its norepinephrine-equivalent dose, antibiotics and hours since the first, supplemental oxygen, invasive ventilation); SOFA over the last 24 hours; age; sex; hours since ICU admission and since recognition. Race and ethnicity are not inputs.
- **Outputs:** probabilities with 90% intervals from patient-level bootstrap refits, and per-input contributions to the log-odds.

## Data

- **Source:** MIMIC-IV version 3.1 (Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, Boston; ICU admissions 2008 to 2022), built into DuckDB with mimic-code. *v0.2:* cohort flow and characteristics from the generated report.
- **Synthetic data (v0.1):** generated by `data/synthetic.py` with the same tables; numbers on synthetic data describe the software, not patients.
- **Outcome:** pre-shock patients, shock (sustained mean arterial pressure below 65 mmHg with lactate above 2 mmol/L, no vasopressor in the definition) or death within 24 hours; shock-stage patients, death within 24 hours. Secondary: death within 28 days.
- **Missing data:** median imputation with "not measured" indicators, fitted on training folds only; measurement age and counts are inputs.

## Development and validation

- Penalty strength chosen by patient-grouped cross-validated log loss inside the training data.
- Internal validation: patient-grouped 5-fold cross-validation stratified by outcome.
- Temporal validation: the most recent admission years (2020 to 2022) held out from fitting.
- Comparison: SOFA and NEWS2, each recalibrated on the same folds.
- External validation: none yet (eICU is planned after v1.0).
- Analysis plan registered on OSF before any model is fitted on MIMIC-IV or the temporal hold-out is evaluated; draft in [analysis_plan.md](analysis_plan.md) (*v0.2*: link to the registration).
- Sensitivity analyses: the culture-antibiotic cohort (`configs/cohort/culture_antibiotic_pair.yaml`) and the Sepsis-3 operational shock outcome (`configs/outcomes/sepsis3_operational_shock.yaml`).

## Performance

*v0.2:* link to the generated report for MIMIC-IV. Example of the report on synthetic data: [reports/synthetic-v0.1/evaluation_report.md](../reports/synthetic-v0.1/evaluation_report.md). ## Fairness and subgroups

Performance is reported by sex, age band (18 to 44, 45 to 64, 65 to 79, 80 and over), race and ethnicity group, and first care unit. Groups too small to report under the small-cell rule are listed as such. *v0.2:* findings and any group with weak performance.

## Limitations

- Predictions describe risk under the care patterns in the training data, not the risk without treatment, and calibration can drift as practice changes.
- Shock is only observable in the ICU, and the shock definition depends on how often lactate is measured.
- Single-centre data; transportability to other hospitals is untested.
- IV fluid volumes are not yet inputs.
- In mimic-code's hourly SOFA, a component with no measurement in the window scores 0, so SOFA can be understated early in a stay.
- Input contributions are associations, not effects.

## Regulatory note

Under the FDA's clinical decision support guidance (January 2026), software that detects sepsis and alerts a clinician is a device function (Example 11), and support for a time-critical decision does not meet Criterion 4. A clinical version would be a medical device. The screen follows Criterion 4's list anyway: intended use, inputs, development and validation, and missing inputs.
